# QA audit: Projects, Lands, Construction (2026-10-11)

Audit only. No source code, schema or behaviour was changed. The findings below are for review;
nothing here has been fixed.

## Scope and method

- **Modules:** Projects (list, form, detail, status pipeline, towers and units, sales card),
  Lands (list, form, detail, status decisions, correction), Construction (list, workspace,
  work breakdown, site log, materials tab, material requests).
- **How:** the running app on `localhost:3000` (demo data, latest `main` + local commits
  `19f4ed8`…`9f09c5b`), driven as Super Admin and as Management, at desktop width and at phone
  width (375 px); plus a read of the repositories and domain rules behind each screen.
- **Evidence column:** *Browser* = reproduced in the app; *Code* = confirmed by reading the code
  path, not executed (used where executing would have destroyed demo bookings).
- **Not in scope:** Bookings, Finance screens (except where Projects feed them), Procurement
  screens, the public portal, and the material-flow batches 4–6 still being built (supplier bills
  screen, Bills to pay), which are known open work rather than defects.

## Projects

### P-01 · Critical · Tower delete and bulk unit delete remove booked and sold units
- **Where:** Project → Towers & Units/Spaces. The bin icon on a tower, and the list view's
  select → Delete. Code: `TowersUnitsPanel.tsx` (`removeCascade`, `bulkRemove`) and
  `project.repository.ts` (`TowerRepository.removeCascade`, `UnitRepository.bulkRemove`).
- **Steps:**
  1. Open PRJ-2026-003 Nokshi Agrabad Trade Centre → Towers & Spaces → list view.
  2. Tick AG-6B, which is **Booked**.
  3. The Delete action is offered. (I did not confirm it, to keep the booking.)
- **Expected:** a unit with a reservation, booking, sale or handover cannot be deleted. Neither
  can a tower holding such units or a site log, the same way the project itself is protected.
- **Actual:** both deletes call `db.units.bulkDelete` with no status check. Tower delete also
  cascades every work item and site-log entry.
- **Impact:** bookings, payment schedules and receipts end up pointing at units that no longer
  exist. The project-level delete guard (QA pass 2026-10-11) is bypassed one level down.

### P-02 · High · A successor project takes the land cost away from the land-share project
- **Where:** Succession card → Start construction project. Code: `projectRepository.startSuccessor`
  moves every `land_project_mapping` row to the new project.
  `financeDashboardRepository.byProject` assigns land cost through those mappings.
- **Expected:** the share project keeps the land it sold. If the land must also show under the
  new project, its cost is not counted twice.
- **Actual:**
  - After succession, the share project has no land, so in Finance it shows full share revenue
    against zero land cost.
  - The new construction project carries the full land cost, even though the shareholders
    already paid for that land.
  - The share project's Land card and land readiness also change to "No land yet".
- **Impact:** the project P&L the owner reads is wrong in both directions for every land-share
  project that goes on to construction.

### P-03 · Medium · Moving back a stage
- **Where:** Project Status card → "Back to …".
- **Steps:** on PRJ-2026-001 (Under Construction, with 14 site updates and material on site), click
  *Back to Approval*.
- **Actual:**
  - The dialog reads "Submit for approval — Record that the design has gone to the approval
    authority". Remarks are optional, and nothing mentions the site log, materials or bookings.
  - Only "Back to Planning" has wording of its own and asks for a reason.
  - Moving back to *Under Construction* (for example from Nearly Complete) re-runs the "start
    construction" rule. `setStatus` writes the dialog date into `actual_start_date`, so entering
    today's date overwrites the real start. Keeping the prefilled date records the move with a
    back-date instead.
- **Expected:** a backward move has its own wording, a required reason, a warning when
  construction records exist, and never rewrites the original actual start.

### P-04 · Medium · Project delete leaves material movements behind
- `projectRepository.deleteBlockReason` checks bookings, purchase orders, expenses and supplier
  payments only.
- A project that received material by dispatch from the main store has no purchase order. It can
  be deleted while it still holds stock rows, transfers, issues, use records and write-offs.
  All of those are left pointing at the deleted project, and the main store's history no longer
  reconciles.

### P-05 · Medium · Sales status can contradict the bookings
- **Observed:**
  - PRJ-2026-003 shows "Sales not open" with an **Open sales** button, while the project list
    shows it at 5% sold with BDT 18,416,800 booked.
  - PRJ-2026-002 is at *Design* with sales open and published. That one is allowed by design
    (warning only) and is listed here for awareness.
- **Cause:** the "sales open" rule is checked only in the booking form (OPEN-ITEMS §0s). The
  repository accepts a booking on a project whose sales are not open, and nothing reconciles the
  two afterwards.

### P-06 · Medium · Project detail page does not fit a phone
- At 375 px wide, `/admin/projects/[id]` measures 513 px wide. The header action row (Open
  construction · Finance · Edit · Delete) does not wrap, so the whole page scrolls sideways.
- Projects list, Lands list and detail, Construction list and workspace, and both forms fit.

### P-07 · Low · The "before approval" warning lifts too early
- `salesOpenChecks` treats the status `approval` as "approval reached". In this pipeline,
  Approval means *submitted to RAJUK/CDA*, so sales open without a warning while the plan is still
  unapproved. That is the case the Real Estate Development and Management Act 2010 warning
  exists for.

### P-08 · Low · Creating a project is not atomic
- `ProjectForm.handleSubmit` saves in three steps: the project, then the picture, then the land
  links. If linking fails (for example, someone else took the land in the meantime):
  - the new project already exists;
  - the form redirects to its edit page, and the error set just before the redirect is lost;
  - there is no `catch`, so a repository error shows nothing at all.

### P-09 · Low · Project list
- The status filter offers Planning / Design / … while cards show each type's own names. A land
  share at Planning is shown as "Register Open", so filtering by "Planning" returns cards that
  never say Planning.
- Land-share and plot cards say "30 units · 30 available" instead of shares or plots.
- Sales status (open / paused / sold out) is not shown on cards and cannot be filtered, though it
  is now a key project fact.

### P-10 · Low · Dates are compulsory too early
- "Construction start" and "Handover target" are required to create a project, even at Planning
  and for an off-plan idea. Users will type placeholder dates, which then drive "behind schedule"
  calculations once work starts.

### P-11 · Low · Actual start editable outside the pipeline
- On edit, "Actual construction start" is shown for every built project whatever its status. It
  can be set on a project still at Planning, contradicting the rule that the stage move stamps it.

### P-12 · Low · Changing project type
- The type is locked only once units exist. A project with towers but no units can switch to
  Land Share and keep its towers and work breakdown.
- Saving as Land Share also clears architect, amenities and actual start without saying so.

### P-13 · Low · Stage-move dates are unchecked
- Stage dates ("Design started on", "Closed on" and the rest) accept any date: in the future, or
  earlier than the previous stage. The Timeline can then show stages out of order.

### P-14 · Low · Wording
- The Projects page subtitle still says "Developments built on acquired or JV-signed land". Since
  v29 a project can exist with no land.
- The Land card hint "All land is owned and needs no further development" is shown for JV land
  (PRJ-2026-001 stands on JV land LND-2026-001).

## Lands

### L-01 · High · "Correct status" bypasses every gate
- **Where:** Land page header → Correct status. Code: `correctableStatuses` (every status except
  On Hold) and `landPipelineRepository.correctStatus` (only checks that a reason was given).
- **Problem:** any land can be set to Acquired, Ready for Project, Rejected or Divested in one
  step. That skips:
  - feasibility approval (G1);
  - mandatory due diligence (G2);
  - the settlement schedule;
  - the rule that a land under a project must be released before it is divested.
- **Who:** the action is visible to every role that can open the page, including view-only
  Management (see X-01).
- **Impact:**
  - Combined with the project rule "construction needs secured land", a land still in
    negotiation can be "corrected" to Acquired to let construction start.
  - A land a project is being built on can be "corrected" to Rejected or Divested.
- **Note:** the correction is logged with a reason. That records the bypass but does not limit
  who can do it or when.

### L-02 · Medium · A land under a project can be put on hold or rejected
- **Steps:** open LND-2026-004 (badge "In project PRJ-2026-010"). The header offers *Put on hold*
  and *Reject land*.
- **Cause:** the page filters out only *Divest* when a project stands on the land, and
  `recordDecision` has no project check at all.
- **Impact:** the project then sits on a rejected land. Its land readiness drops to "Land in
  acquisition" with no explanation on the project.

### L-03 · Medium · Land form validation gaps
- **Steps:** Add Land → Joint Venture, with developer 100 %, landowner 0 %, agreement date
  2030-01-01, GPS 999 / −500, and no landowner. Save.
- **Actual:** the land saved (as LND-2026-021, since deleted). The detail page plotted
  "999.00000, -500.00000" on the map and showed "JV split: Developer 100% · Owner 0%".
  - Shares outside 0–100 are stopped only by the browser's number limits. `validate()` and
    `recordJvSigning` only check that the two shares total 100.
  - A JV at the *sourcing* stage must already have an "Agreement Date", although signing is a
    later pipeline step ("JV signed" on the Progress card). Users will invent a date.
- **Expected:**
  - each JV share strictly between 0 and 100;
  - latitude within ±90 and longitude within ±180, ideally a Bangladesh-range warning;
  - no future agreement date;
  - the agreement date asked for at signing, not at creation;
  - at least one landowner for a JV, or a visible warning.

### L-04 · Medium · The land list does not show project links
- The list cards carry no "In project PRJ-…" badge; the detail page does.
- Lands already carrying a project still show the status "Ready for Project" (for example
  LND-2026-020 under PRJ-2026-009, and LND-2026-001 under PRJ-2026-001). The land team cannot
  tell which "ready" plots are actually free.

### L-05 · Low · Duplicate land names are allowed
- Project names are checked for duplicates (case and spaces ignored). Land names are not, so two
  "Bashundhara Block K corner plot" records can exist and be confused in the land picker.

### L-06 · Low · Creation is not in the status history
- A new land's "Status changes" card reads "No status changes yet". Its first status (Sourced, or
  Acquired for owned land) is only on the record for owned entries.

### L-07 · Low · Pipeline sections are hard to find
- The tabs are Overview / Owners / Joint Venture / Documents / Timeline. Site Visit, Due
  Diligence, Negotiation & Acquisition and Development open only from the Progress card buttons
  or inside Timeline.
- The help text says "Registration is recorded under Negotiation & Acquisition", which is not a
  visible tab.

## Construction

### C-01 · High · A future-dated reading overrides every genuine later reading
- **Steps:**
  1. Construction → Log Update → Nokshi Uttara Heights → Main Building → Foundation.
  2. Enter date 2027-06-15 and 40 %. Save. It is accepted.
  3. Log another reading for the same item dated today, at 10 %. Save.
  4. The item still reads 40 %.
- **Cause:**
  - The date field's `max` attribute is only a hint; the dialog saves from a button click, not a
    form submit, so the browser never enforces it.
  - `applyLatestReading` takes the reading with the latest `update_date`, so the 2027 reading wins
    until mid-2027.
- **Impact:** one mistyped year freezes an item's progress, its tower %, the project % and the
  behind-schedule flags, and every later genuine report is silently ignored. The dialog even
  said "Was 40%".
- **Expected:**
  - refuse dates after today;
  - consider also refusing dates before the project's actual start;
  - when a back-dated reading is entered, say whether it will become the current figure.

### C-02 · Medium · Progress can be logged on projects not under construction
- **Steps:** Construction list → Log Update. The Project list offers all 10 projects: Planning,
  Design, Approval, both land shares and the plot scheme.
- **Actual:**
  - A reading was saved against PRJ-2026-002, which is at *Design*.
  - Demo data already shows the effect: PRJ-2026-003 at *Approval* has Block 1 at 2.25 %, and its
    tower status reads "Under Construction" on the project page.
- **Expected:** only projects whose construction has started (the same rule the Construction list
  uses).

### C-03 · Medium · Deleting a work item erases its history
- `towerWorkItemRepository.removeCascade` hard-deletes every site-log entry and photo for the
  item. The dialog warns about this.
- It clears `work_item_id` on material requests but not on `stock_issues` or
  `stock_consumptions`. Material recorded as used "for Tower A · Superstructure" then points at
  a deleted item.
- The button is not role-gated (X-01). Months of daily logs are one click away for anyone who
  can open the page.
- **Suggested direction (for review):** archive or retire a work item instead of deleting it once
  it has readings or material use.

### C-04 · Medium · Weights do not have to total 100 %
- Each weight is checked to be 0–100, but the tower total is not. An unbalanced breakdown only
  shows a warning.
- `towerProgressPct` does not cap the result. A tower whose weights total 150 % reports up to
  150 %, and one totalling 80 % can never pass 80 %. Both feed project readiness: the handover and
  close checks, and the public progress figure.

### C-05 · Medium · Material request form and lifecycle
- The Project list offers every project, not only those under construction.
- A line with an item but no quantity, or quantity 0, is silently dropped. The request saves with
  fewer lines than were typed.
- The same item can appear on two lines.
- Edit and Delete stay available after approval and ordering:
  - Editing changes requested quantities behind the PM's decision.
  - Deleting a request that has purchase orders leaves those orders' `request_id` pointing at
    nothing (`materialRequestRepository.removeCascade` has no check).

### C-06 · Low · Misleading "no longer exists"
- `/admin/site-progress/<id>` for a project that exists but is not under construction (for example
  PRJ-2026-003 or a land share) says "This project no longer exists."
- Deleting a reading on such a project (possible because of C-02) redirects to that page.

### C-07 · Low · No remark required when progress goes down
- The dialog says "lower than the last reading — the item will move back", but the reason stays
  optional.

### C-08 · Low · Demo data
- PRJ-2026-007 Nokshi Lakeview Residence, the "every field filled" showcase, has been under
  construction since 18 May 2026. It has no site log, so it opens as "Never reported · Behind
  Schedule · 0 %".

## Across the modules

### X-01 · High · Role permissions are not enforced on these screens
- **Steps:** switch the demo session to **Management**, which has *view* rights on Project, Land
  and Site Progress in the permission matrix.
- **Actual:**
  - **Project page:** Edit, Delete, Move to Nearly Complete, Back to Approval, Pause sales and
    Mark sold out are all shown.
  - **Land page:** Put on hold, Reject land, Correct status, Edit and Delete are shown.
  - **Construction workspace:** Log Update, Log progress, Edit and Delete work item are shown.
- **Code:** `canEdit` / `canApprove` appear in neither `projects/[id]` nor `lands/[id]` (only for
  owner amounts), nor in the work breakdown or site-log pages.
- The Materials tab, the request status card and the Procurement screens *are* gated, so the
  behaviour is inconsistent.
- **Impact:** the Section 9.6 matrix is only enforced at the menu and route level. Once a page
  opens, every action works.

### X-02 · Low · Codes are reused after a delete
- `nextCode` takes the highest existing number + 1. Deleting the newest land, project or request
  frees its code for the next record. An LND-2026-021 in an old email or printout can then mean
  a different plot.

## Improvement opportunities (not defects)

1. **Reversible instead of destructive deletes** for towers, work items and readings once anything
   depends on them: archive, retire, or "void with reason".
2. **One "Land in use" indicator** on land cards, the land picker and land statuses:
   Ready (free) vs In project.
3. **A Construction start checklist on the project:** land secured, towers and work breakdown
   balanced to 100 %, planned dates inside the project window.
4. **A site-log sanity line** in the dialog: "this will become the current reading / this is
   older than the current reading".
5. **A sales consistency check:** when a booking exists on a project whose sales are not open,
   say so on the project and offer to open sales.
6. **A project list sales chip and filter**, plus type-specific stage names in the status filter.
7. **Settle "Correct status" ownership:** management or super admin only, never onto or off a
   land a project stands on, and show corrections distinctly on the land timeline.
8. **Optional dates at Planning**, required from Approval onwards.
9. **A Lakeview site log in the demo**, so the showcase project looks like a live site.

## Scenarios still worth testing (not covered here)

- Every land sub-panel end to end: site visits, feasibility versions, the DD checklist and
  waivers, negotiation rounds, settlement schedule, registration, JV signing, development
  activities. These were code-scanned only.
- Unit generation patterns (flats, plots, shares) at volume, and editing towers after generation.
- Two users in two tabs: concurrent code generation (`&code` unique index), a land picked by two
  project forms, and a status moved while another user's dialog is open.
- Large demo volumes: every list loads whole tables (`toArray`) and filters in memory.
- Accessibility: keyboard-only use of the custom ⋯ menus and dialogs, and screen-reader labels on
  icon buttons.
- The public portal's view of a project after the defects above: a tower at 150 %, or progress on
  a Design-stage project.
